## Interpret actual-state updates correctly

- Keep **project progress**, **actual payment**, and **original contract terms** separate. Completion never proves full payment, signing, preparation, acceptance, invoicing, or refund completion.
- Give the user's explicit confirmation of actual completion/payment precedence over older contracts and documents. Preserve original contractual terms separately and flag conflicts for reconciliation. Do not revert confirmed facts because a draft differs, a deadline passed, or no later email was found.
- Preserve unknowns as unknown or blank according to the schema. Distinguish unknown from zero, unpaid, overdue, and not applicable. Do not silently convert draft terms into signed obligations or an unsigned document into a signed contract.
- Treat third-party email/payment claims as reported or proposed until corroborated by reliable evidence or confirmed by the user. Do not automatically mark a receipt, signature, preparation, or completion from a filename, draft, payment schedule, elapsed date, or absence of contrary evidence. Surface ambiguous actual-state changes for review.
- Interpret an unqualified user “done” as `Project status = Completed`, `Reminders = Off`, and no active next action. Retain the project and history. Leave unmentioned payments and individual milestones unchanged; if payment is unknown, say so without reopening follow-up.
- Preserve `Completed` and `Reminders = Off` during discovery and scheduled sync. Reopen a project or enable reminders only on a clear user instruction for that project, including an explicit instruction read during a scheduled check; otherwise propose reopening. A new copy or changed contract does not reopen it.
- For deposits/partial payments, capture the reported percentage or amount, currency, total basis, payment channel, and source. Use only a verified applicable total to calculate deposit and arithmetic remaining amount. Label calculations explicitly; they do not prove receipt, settlement, invoicing, VAT treatment, or a refund obligation.
- If a total, currency, percentage basis, invoice/VAT state, or whether a payment is additional is uncertain, preserve confirmed facts and ask the smallest necessary question before dependent arithmetic or a total change. Do not infer that “50% deposit” is another 50% on top of an existing deposit.
- Preserve “private account” exactly as a reported channel. Do not infer account ownership, account details, actual payment date, invoice status, or refund arrangements. Never store credentials or full bank/card secrets in the tracker or log.

- Check delivered reminder history when available. Suppress unchanged reminders; repeat only when the due date arrives, facts materially change, or delay creates material new risk.
